Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 8, 2026

Introduction

PixelMe ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information our mobile application PixelMe - Pixel Art Creator (the "App") handles and how.

Information We Collect

Photos and Images

  • The App reads only the photos you explicitly pick for pixelation
  • All photo processing happens locally on your device
  • We do NOT upload, store, or transmit your photos to our servers or any third party
  • When you save your work, the App asks for permission to add images to your Photos library; it does not read the rest of your library

In-App Purchase Information

  • Pro is available as a weekly, monthly, or yearly subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase; Apple processes every transaction
  • The App only checks with Apple whether your Apple Account owns an active Pro purchase
  • We do NOT receive or store your payment information

Feedback You Send

  • If you choose to send feedback from the App's support section, what you write (feedback type, message, any screenshots you attach, and a name or email if you enter one for a reply) is delivered to us through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit), together with the app version, device model, iOS version and language setting
  • Nothing is sent unless you send feedback yourself. Sending requires an iCloud sign-in; you can also email us instead
  • We use feedback only to reply and to improve the App

Analytics Data

  • We do NOT use any third-party analytics or advertising services
  • We do NOT track your usage behavior or collect telemetry data

How We Use Your Information

  • Photos: Processed locally on your device to create pixel art effects
  • Purchase Status: To unlock Pro features for verified purchasers
  • Feedback: To answer you and fix problems you report

Data Storage and Security

  • All photos and created pixel art remain on your device
  • We do NOT maintain any servers or cloud storage for user content
  • Your data is protected by your device's security features and Apple's iOS security measures

Third-Party Services

The App does NOT integrate with any third-party services except Apple's own:

  • Apple App Store: For app distribution and in-app purchases
  • Apple iCloud (CloudKit): Only to deliver feedback you choose to send

Both are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do NOT share your photos or data with third parties.

Your Rights

  • Access: Your data stays on your device; you have full access at all times
  • Deletion: Uninstall the App to remove all associated data from your device. To have feedback you sent deleted, email us
  • Opt-Out: You can change photo permissions at any time in iOS Settings
  • Portability: Export your created pixel art at any time

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages (4+). We do NOT knowingly collect personal information from children. Photo processing happens entirely on-device, and the App collects no personal data on its own.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted with a new "Last Updated" date.
