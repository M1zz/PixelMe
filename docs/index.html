Turn a photo into a pattern

Pick a photo of your pet, your face or a favorite character. Hantam turns it into a fuse bead or cross-stitch pattern, matched to colors you can actually buy.

Real colors

Perler, Hama and Artkal beads and DMC thread. Every square shows a real color number.

Know what to buy

See how many beads or stitches each color needs, and copy a shopping list.

Board by board

Big patterns split into 29 × 29 pegboards, with bold board lines.

Fix any square

Tap a square to change it, or swap one color for another everywhere.

Print and make

With Pro, export a printable PDF: a color list plus symbol charts, page by page.

Pixel art too

Pixelate photos, draw along with 110 samples, and animate frame by frame.

Hantam Pro

Free lets you make patterns up to one pegboard (or 40 stitches), keep 3 patterns and share images with a small mark. Pro adds printable PDFs, patterns up to 4 × 4 boards or 200 stitches, unlimited saved patterns, images without the mark and every pixel art tool. Choose a monthly or yearly subscription, or pay once for lifetime access.
